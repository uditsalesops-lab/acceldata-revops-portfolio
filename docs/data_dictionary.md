# Data dictionary: synthetic Acceldata CRM dataset

Generated by `scripts/generate_data.py` (seed 42, so every run gives the same data).
All data is fake. Dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`. Money is USD. Data covers 1 Apr 2025 to 8 Oct 2026 (6 quarters + current).

**Owner** = the team accountable for keeping the field correct in a real company:
RevOps (systems and data), Sales (reps and managers), Marketing Ops (campaigns and leads), Finance.

## Known data-quality issues (planted on purpose)

| Issue | Where | Size | Fixed in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Duplicate contacts (upper-case email, short first name, trailing space) | contacts | ~15% (about 900 rows) | P1, P8 |
| Missing industry | accounts.industry | 8% | P1, P5 |
| Bad job titles (`x`, `N/A`, `data guy`…) with no persona | contacts.job_title, persona | 10% | P1, P5 |
| EU contacts missing GDPR lawful basis | contacts.lawful_basis | 3% of EU contacts | P1 |
| Personal email leads (gmail, outlook, yahoo) | leads.email | about 12% | P4 |
| Lead company name typed differently from the account (`Inc`, `GmbH`, `Ltd`, `Group` added) | leads.company | about 18% of leads from existing accounts | P4 |
| Leads with no owner | leads.owner_id | 5% | P4 |
| Deals that skip a stage | opportunity_history | about 12% of deals | P2 |
| Slipped close dates (pushed 1–3 times) | opportunities.close_date_push_count | about 45% of deals | P6 |
| Open deals with a close date in the past | opportunities.close_date | a few rows | P6 |
| Open deals with no activity in 21+ days | activities | about 15% of open deals | P6, P7 |
| Accounts owned by an inactive rep | users.is_active = false | 1 rep | P4 |

**Lead sources (shared list):** SDR Outbound, Event, Webinar, Paid Search, Content Download, Partner, Free Trial, Referral.

**How the tables join**

- accounts.account_id → contacts, opportunities, activities
- users.user_id → accounts.account_owner_id, leads.owner_id, opportunities.owner_id / se_id, activities.owner_id
- campaigns.campaign_id → leads.campaign_id, opportunities.primary_campaign_id
- opportunities.opportunity_id → opportunity_history, activities
- leads.lead_id → activities (prospecting touches)
- leads.converted_contact_id → contacts, leads.converted_account_id → accounts (lead conversion: the full chain is campaign → lead → contact → account → opportunity)
